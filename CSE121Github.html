Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our school and courses

How do I enroll in a course? +

Enrolling is simple! Browse our courses section, click the "Enroll" button on the course you're interested in, fill out the enrollment form, and complete the payment. You'll get instant access to all course materials.

What is the duration of each course? +

Course durations vary depending on the subject matter. Most courses range from 2 to 4 months, with classes typically held 3-4 times per week. You can check the specific duration for each course in the courses section.

Do you offer certificates upon completion? +

Yes! Upon successful completion of any course, you'll receive a recognized certificate of completion. These certificates can boost your professional profile and are valued by employers and educational institutions.

Is there a refund policy? +

We offer a 100% refund within 7 days of enrollment if you're not satisfied with the course. After 7 days, a partial refund may be available depending on course progress. Contact our support team for details.

Do you provide support outside class hours? +

Absolutely! We offer 24/7 online support through email, chat, and phone. Our dedicated support team is always ready to help you with any questions or technical issues you might encounter.

Can I switch courses after enrolling? +

Yes, you can switch courses within the first 14 days of enrollment without any penalty. Simply log in to your account, navigate to "My Courses," and select the new course you'd like to take.

Are there any prerequisites for courses? +

Most courses are designed for all levels. However, some advanced courses may require previous knowledge. Each course page clearly lists any prerequisites. If you're unsure, our advisors can help you choose the right course.

How can I contact a teacher outside of class? +

You can reach out to teachers through the course forum or send them an email through the platform. Teachers typically respond within 24 hours. You can also schedule one-on-one sessions during their office hours.
